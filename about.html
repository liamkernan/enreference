An open cultural index

One subject per issue, read through the things around it: a record, a place, a picture.

Every issue comes with four games. Frame of reference: name it from a detail. Needle drop: name the record from a second of sound. Where + when: pin the place, date the moment. Final frame: name the film from its last shot.

Listings cover Los Angeles and New York.
